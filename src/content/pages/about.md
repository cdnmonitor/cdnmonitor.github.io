I am an engineer based in Los Angeles. My work sits where software meets the
physical: firmware for devices, CAM and fixturing for parts that have to be
made more than once, and the tooling that keeps a design and its documentation
in agreement.

I studied Computer Science at Chapman University and have run wpm design since
2020, building products end to end: schematic, board, enclosure, firmware,
and the software around them.
