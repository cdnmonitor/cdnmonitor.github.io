Any renders, scans, product photos, PCB art, packaging was designed by myself. I like to
employ mixing 3d art with 2d filters like dithers and bitmaps like on the type65
pcb, or create blender filters to make renders look like line art like in the
packaging. Ultimately my preference for art often involves making 2D art look 3D,
or making 3D art 2D.

No generative art is used. Every graphic is shot, drawn or retouched by hand, and
what is on the site is the object.
