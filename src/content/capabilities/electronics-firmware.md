During my time as a student at Chapman I took a few classes that involved
breadboard wiring and later on a PCB design class. Afterwards, as paid faculty
teaching pinouts, soldering, wiring and chassis help, I have helped countless
students design their own wiring, and later, worked on wiring on wpm products. We
have also outsourced PCBs during wpm and I have worked alongside EEs to make sure
software challenges like USB enumeration, lights, matrices, and response time were
optimal. I also wrote wpm firmware and its interface with the wpm desktop app.
