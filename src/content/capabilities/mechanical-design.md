Many projects and jobs have required Fusion 360 from designing a fiber laser
enclosure, to designing trading card scales, to designing lasering jigs, focusing
tools, and wpm products. I am able to design based off whatever type of machine is
being used, whether a laser, a CNC, or a 3D printer and know each of their
individual limitations.

The same model goes to the printer first and the mill second, so the cheap
mistakes happen before the expensive ones. See [wpm design](/experience/wpm-design/)
for how that runs as a company.
