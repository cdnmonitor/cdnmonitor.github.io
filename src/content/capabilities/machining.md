I operated and maintained a CNC Machine, a 60W Fiber Laser, Bambulabs and Prusa
filament printers, Heygears and Creality resin printers in my workshop alone. In my
other paid jobs I have operated and maintained Roland 4th axis mills, Tormach
mills, Formlabs printers, CO2 lasers, and many other types of fabrication tools.

Finishing is part of the same loop: what comes off the machine still has to look
like a product.
