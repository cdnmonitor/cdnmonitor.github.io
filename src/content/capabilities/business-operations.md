I have learned from my entrepreneurial pasts at my own expense. From operating a
SaaS to B2B clothing manufacturing to a hardware startup I have learned how to
operate businesses with a lean mindset ensuring no volatile markets affect the
product.

I also have done the labor at every step to understand the difficulties of the job
and understanding the value that is brought. I also make spreadsheets to decide
prices, margins, and account for metal markets and new tariffs.
